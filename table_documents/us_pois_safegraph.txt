Description: The us_pois_safegraph table contains information about Points of Interest (POIs) in the United States. Each record represents a POI with specific attributes including location_name, street_address, city, region, postal_code, geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude), categories (top_category, sub_category, and category_tags), business contact information (phone_number), and additional information such as operating hours, polygon information, and whether the POI includes a parking lot. The primary functionality of this table is to provide a comprehensive dataset of POIs across the United States for various applications.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications for the us_pois_safegraph table include geographic data analysis and visualization, business directory services, mapping applications and navigation systems, location-based recommendations, market research, infrastructure planning, and emergency response planning.

Keywords: Points of Interest, POI, United States, locations, geographic data, restaurants, businesses, street addresses, geo-coordinates, mapping, navigation, business directories.

Column descriptions for us_pois_safegraph table:
- us_pois_safegraph.safegraph_place_id: A unique identifier for each point of interest location, assigned by Safegraph.
- us_pois_safegraph.parent_safegraph_place_id: A unique identifier for the parent location of a point of interest, assigned by Safegraph.
- us_pois_safegraph.safegraph_brand_ids: The brand identifier(s) associated with a point of interest, assigned by Safegraph.
- us_pois_safegraph.location_name: The name of the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.brands: The associated brand name(s).
- us_pois_safegraph.top_category: A high-level category describing the industry of the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.sub_category: A more specific category describing the industry of the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.naics_code: A numerical code assigned to the point of interest based on the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS).
- us_pois_safegraph.latitude: The latitude coordinate of the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.longitude: The longitude coordinate of the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.street_address: The street address of the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.primary_number: The primary street number associated with the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.street_predirection: The directional indicator that precedes a street name.
- us_pois_safegraph.street_name: The name of the street on which the point of interest is located.
- us_pois_safegraph.street_postdirection: The directional indicator that follows a street name.
- us_pois_safegraph.street_suffix: The suffix of a street name (e.g. "St", "Rd", "Ave").
- us_pois_safegraph.city: The city in which the point of interest is located.
- us_pois_safegraph.region: The state or region in which the point of interest is located.
- us_pois_safegraph.postal_code: The postal code associated with the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.open_hours: The hours of operation for the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.category_tags: Additional descriptive tags for the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.polygon_wkt: A polygon representation of the shape of the point of interest boundary.
- us_pois_safegraph.polygon_class: An identifier indicating whether the polygon shape is owned or shared.
- us_pois_safegraph.phone_number: The phone number associated with the point of interest.
- us_pois_safegraph.is_synthetic: A flag indicating whether the point of interest is a synthetic location created for privacy or sampling purposes.
- us_pois_safegraph.includes_parking_lot: A flag indicating whether a parking lot is included in the point of interest boundary.
- us_pois_safegraph.iso_country_code: The ISO country code for the country in which the point of interest is located.
