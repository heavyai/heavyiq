Description:
The aishub_2021_06_16 table contains information about vessels and their movements at specific timestamps. Its primary functionality is to store vessel details such as identification, locations (latitude and longitude), and navigational information like course, speed, and heading. Moreover, secondary functionality includes keeping information about vessel attributes such as name, call sign, type, device type, dimensions, destination, ETA (estimated arrival time), and position accuracy.

Applications:
This table can be used in various applications, including:
1. Monitoring maritime traffic and detecting unusual vessel activities.
2. Analyzing vessel movement patterns and routes for research or management purposes.
3. Assisting in search and rescue operations for missing or distressed vessels.
4. Ensuring maritime regulations are followed through automatic identification system (AIS) compliance checks.
5. Providing insights and recommendations on optimizing shipping routes and schedules to shipping companies.

Keywords:
Vessel, Automatic Identification System (AIS), maritime traffic, navigation information, latitude, longitude, course, speed, heading, ETA (estimated arrival time), position accuracy, destination, call sign, device type, vessel dimensions

- aishub_2021_06_16.position_accuracy: The accuracy of the reported position data
