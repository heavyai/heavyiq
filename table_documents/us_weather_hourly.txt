Description: The us_weather_hourly table is designed to store hourly weather data from various weather stations across the United States. The primary functionality of this table is to record and organize weather-related information such as temperature, precipitation, solar radiation, surface temperature, humidity, and soil moisture and temperature at different depths. The secondary functionality is to provide details about each weather station, including its full name, state, location, number, direction, and observation time.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications for the us_weather_hourly table include:

1. Weather forecasting: By analyzing historical weather data, meteorologists can develop more accurate weather forecasts and identify trends and patterns.
2. Climate research: Researchers can use this data to study climate change and its impact on various regions in the United States.
3. Agriculture: Farmers can utilize this information to make informed decisions about planting, irrigation, and harvesting based on weather conditions and soil moisture levels.
4. Renewable energy: Solar and wind energy companies can analyze solar radiation and wind data to determine the optimal locations for installing renewable energy infrastructure.
5. Emergency management: Government agencies can use this data to prepare for and respond to natural disasters such as floods, droughts, and heatwaves.
