Description: The Electric_Power_Transmission_Lines table stores information about electric power transmission lines, including their circuit type, comments, legend, length in feet and miles, name, object ID, owner, shape length, status, type, and various environmental and risk factors. The primary functionality of this table is to store and manage data related to electric power transmission lines, while the secondary functionality is to provide insights into environmental factors and risks associated with these lines.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications for this table include:
1. Power grid management: Utility companies can use this data to monitor and manage their power transmission lines, ensuring efficient and reliable power distribution.
2. Infrastructure planning: Government agencies and utility companies can use this data to plan for future infrastructure development and upgrades.
3. Risk assessment: Utility companies and emergency management agencies can use the environmental and risk factors to assess potential hazards and develop strategies to mitigate them.
4. Asset management: Utility companies can use this data to track the status and ownership of transmission lines, facilitating maintenance and repair activities.
5. Geographic analysis: Researchers and analysts can use the geographic data to study the spatial distribution of power transmission lines and their relationship with other environmental factors.

Keywords: electric power, transmission lines, infrastructure, power grid, risk assessment, environmental factors, asset management, geographic analysis.
