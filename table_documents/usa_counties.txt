Description: The usa_counties table is designed to store demographic, geographic, and socioeconomic data for counties across the United States. The primary functionality of this table is to provide a comprehensive dataset for analyzing various aspects of the population, such as age distribution, race, and household composition. Additionally, the table includes information on housing units, agricultural data, and geographic attributes, such as area and shape, which serve as secondary functionalities.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications for the usa_counties table include:
1. Analyzing population trends and demographic shifts across different regions of the United States.
2. Identifying areas with specific demographic characteristics for targeted marketing or policy implementation.
3. Examining the relationship between population density, housing, and agricultural land use.
4. Conducting geographic analysis and visualization of county-level data for research or decision-making purposes.

Keywords: demographics, population, age distribution, race, households, housing units, agriculture, geographic analysis, United States, counties, socioeconomic data, land use, geographic attributes
