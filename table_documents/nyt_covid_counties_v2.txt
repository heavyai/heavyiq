Description: The nyt_covid_counties_v2 table primarily stores COVID-19 data for various counties and states in the United States on a daily basis. It includes information such as the number of confirmed cases, deaths, and their percentages within the population. Additionally, it provides lagged data (both 1-day and 7-day) on new cases and new deaths, as well as the daily averages of cases and deaths as a percentage of the population infected.

Applications: This table can be used for various purposes, such as analyzing the trends in COVID-19 cases and deaths, comparing the impact of the virus across different regions, analyzing the efficacy of public health measures, and informing data-driven decision-making for governments, healthcare providers, businesses, and individuals. It can also be used for predictive modeling, risk assessments, and evaluating the progress towards herd immunity.

Keywords: COVID-19, county, state, cases, deaths, population, percentage, new cases, new deaths, lag, daily average, infection rate, mortality rate, trends, comparison, public health measures, risk assessment, predictive modeling, herd immunity.

Column descriptions for nyt_covid_counties_v2 table:
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.reporting_date: The date for which the COVID data is being reported.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.county: The name of the county to which the COVID data belongs.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.state: The name of the state to which the county belongs.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.fips: The FIPS code that identifies the state and county combination.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.cases: The total number of COVID cases in the county as of the reporting date.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.deaths: The total number of COVID deaths in the county as of the reporting date.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.total_case_pct_per_pop: The percentage of the total county population that has tested positive for COVID as of the reporting date.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.total_mortality_pct_per_pop: The percentage of the total county population that has died due to COVID as of the reporting date.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.new_cases_1_day_lag: The total number of new COVID cases in the county in the last 24 hours.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.new_cases_7_day_lag: The total number of new COVID cases in the county in the last 7 days.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.new_deaths_1_day_lag: The total number of new COVID deaths in the county in the last 24 hours.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.new_deaths_7_day_lag: The total number of new COVID deaths in the county in the last 7 days.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.daily_cases_pct_pop_infected_1_day_avg: The average percentage of the county population that has been infected with COVID on a daily basis for the past 24 hours.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.daily_cases_pct_pop_infected_7_day_avg: The average percentage of the county population that has been infected with COVID on a daily basis for the past 7 days.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.daily_death_pct_pop_infected_1_day_avg: The average percentage of the county population that has died due to COVID on a daily basis for the past 24 hours.
- nyt_covid_counties_v2.daily_death_pct_pop_infected_7_day_avg: The average percentage of the county population that has died due to COVID on a daily basis for the past 7 days.
