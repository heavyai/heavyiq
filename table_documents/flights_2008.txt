Description: The flights_2008 table contains detailed information about domestic flights in the United States during the year 2008. The primary functionality of this table is to store flight data, such as departure and arrival times, carrier details, and delay information. Secondary functionality includes providing information about airports, aircraft details, such as type and manufacturer, and geography (latitude and longitude coordinates).

Applications: This table can be used for a variety of applications, including but not limited to:
1. Analyzing travel patterns, airline performance, and identifying potential improvements in operational efficiency.
2. Performing historical analysis of flight delays, cancellations, and possible factors contributing to these issues.
3. Investigating relationships between airport locations, carriers, and aircraft types.
4. Enabling efficient route and connection planning for airlines, passengers, and logistical operations.
5. Creating insightful visualizations and dashboards for stakeholders in the aviation industry.

Keywords: flights, airlines, carriers, airports, aircraft, delay, cancellation, departure, arrival, performance, 2008, United States, domestic, travel patterns, operational efficiency, route planning, visualization.

- flight_year: year of the flight (e.g. 2008)
- flight_month: month of the flight (1-12)
- flight_dayofmonth: day of the month of the flight (1-31)
- flight_dayofweek: day of the week of the flight (1-7, where 1 is Monday, 7 is Sunday)
- deptime: scheduled departure time (HHMM format)
- crsdeptime: expected departure time (HHMM format)
- arrtime: scheduled arrival time (HHMM format)
- crsarrtime: expected arrival time (HHMM format)
- uniquecarrier: unique carrier code
- flightnum: flight number
- tailnum: tail number of the aircraft
- actualelapsedtime: actual elapsed time of the flight (in minutes)
- crselapsedtime: expected elapsed time of the flight (in minutes)
- airtime: actual time spent in the air (in minutes)
- arrdelay: arrival delay (in minutes)
- depdelay: departure delay (in minutes)
- origin: three-letter code of the origin airport
- dest: three-letter code of the destination airport
- distance: distance between airports (in miles)
- taxiin: taxi in time (in minutes)
- taxiout: taxi out time (in minutes)
- cancelled: whether the flight was cancelled (1) or not (0)
- cancellationcode: reason for cancellation (A, B, C, D)
- diverted: whether the flight was diverted (1) or not (0)
- carrierdelay: delay caused by the carrier (in minutes)
- weatherdelay: delay caused by weather (in minutes)
- nasdelay: delay caused by the National Airspace System (in minutes)
- securitydelay: delay caused by security (in minutes)
- lateaircraftdelay: delay caused by a previous flight being late (in minutes)
- dep_timestamp: actual departure timestamp
- arr_timestamp: actual arrival timestamp
- carrier_name: name of the carrier
- plane_type: type of plane (corporation, individual, foreign corporation, co-owner, or partnership)
- plane_manufacturer: manufacturer of the plane (Boeing, Bombardier Inc, Embraer, Airbus Industrie, McDonnell Douglas)
- plane_issue_date: date when the plane was issued
- plane_model: model of the plane (CL-600-2B19, 737-7H4, EMB-145LR, 737-3H4, A320-232)
- plane_status: current status of the plane (Valid or Registered to Mfr)
- plane_aircraft_type: type of aircraft (fixed wing multi-engine, fixed wing single-engine, balloon or rotorcraft)
- plane_engine_type: type of engine (turbo-fan, turbo-jet, turbo-prop, reciprocating or none)
- plane_year: year when the plane was manufactured
- origin_name: name of the origin airport
- origin_city: city of the origin airport
- origin_state: state of the origin airport
- origin_country: country of the origin airport
- origin_lat: latitude of the origin airport
- origin_lon: longitude of the origin airport
- dest_name: name of the destination airport
- dest_city: city of the destination airport
- dest_state: state of the destination airport
- dest_country: country of the destination airport
- dest_lat: latitude of the destination airport
- dest_lon: longitude of the destination airport
