Description: The zillow_recent_sales_full_peninsula table stores information about recently sold residential properties in the Peninsula region. The table contains details such as property ID, address, sale price, sale date, property type, and various other attributes related to property's features and location. Primary functionality of the table is to maintain a record of property sales data, while the secondary functionality includes providing details on property attributes and facilitating property search and analysis.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications for the data stored in this table include:
1. Real estate market analysis to understand recent sales trends, pricing, and property types in the Peninsula region.
2. Generating statistics and reports to assist potential buyers, sellers, and real estate professionals in making data-driven decisions.
3. Developing property valuation models and tools, such as Zestimate, to help estimate the market value of a property.
4. Facilitating search and retrieval of recently sold properties by features, price, location, or other desired criteria.

Keywords: Zillow, recent sales, Peninsula region, real estate, property attributes, property search, market analysis, residential properties, property valuation, Zestimate.
