Description:
The "zillow_recent_sales_full_peninsula" table is a comprehensive database containing information about recently sold properties in the Peninsula region. This table stores crucial details such as property address, sale price, date sold, and property type. Primary functionalities of this table include tracking recent property transactions and providing insights into property features such as the number of bedrooms, bathrooms, and living area size. Secondary functionalities comprise analyzing property-related aspects such as image availability, video content, whether the user claims property ownership, or whether the property is non-owner occupied.

Applications:
This table can be used for various applications, such as:
1. Real estate market analysis: Assessing the property sales trends, average selling prices, most popular property types, and identifying high-demand areas in the Peninsula region.
2. Property valuation: By analyzing similar properties and their sold prices, property valuation estimates can be derived. 
3. Investment opportunities: Evaluating potential real estate investment opportunities for profitability, based on property features, location, and historical sale data.
4. Statistical analysis: Observing patterns and correlations between different property characteristics, such as the relationship between living area size and sale price or address proximity to schools or transport links.
