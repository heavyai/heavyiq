Description: The cell_towers_us table contains information about cell towers present in the United States. The primary functionality of the table is to store details about each cell tower, including the type of radio technology used, the network and area it belongs to, its geographical location in terms of longitude, latitude, and geometrical point, and other relevant characteristics such as range, number of samples taken, changeability, creation and modification timestamps, and average signal strength. This enables users to analyze and understand the distribution and performance of cell towers across the country.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications of the cell_towers_us table include:

1. Network coverage analysis: By analyzing the density and distribution of cell towers, network operators or service providers can identify areas with weak or no network coverage and work on improving the network infrastructure in those areas.
2. Performance benchmarking: Analyzing the average signal strength across multiple towers or networks can help identify areas that require performance optimization or identify factors affecting the performance of specific towers.
3. Cell tower clustering: Identifying clusters of cell towers can help optimize the use of resources, workload distribution, and network management, enabling efficient operation and maintenance.
4. Geospatial analysis: Analyzing the geographical distribution of cell towers can aid in understanding regional variations in network infrastructure, coverage, and signal strength, and can be used to inform network planning and expansion efforts.

Keywords: cell tower, network coverage, performance analysis, geospatial analysis, infrastructure, optimization, distribution, signal strength, range, coverage analysis, network operator, service provider, cell cluster, tower management

Column descriptions for cell_towers_us table:
- cell_towers_us.radio: Radio access technology used by the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.mcc: Mobile country code of the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.net: Network code of the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.area: Geographic area code of the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.cell: Unique identifier of the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.unit: Unit identifier of the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.lon: Longitude of the location of the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.lat: Latitude of the location of the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.location: Geometry data type that represents the location of the cell tower as a point in WGS84 projection.
- cell_towers_us.range_: Approximate range of the cell tower in meters.
- cell_towers_us.samples: Number of samples collected from the cell tower.
- cell_towers_us.changeable: Flag that determines if the cell tower is subject to change (1) or not (0).
- cell_towers_us.created: Timestamp indicating when the cell tower was created.
- cell_towers_us.updated: Timestamp indicating when the cell tower was last updated.
- cell_towers_us.average_signal: Average signal value of the cell tower.
