Description: The florida_parcels_2020 table contains data about various parcels of land in Florida. It includes information on parcel use, owner details, location, property values, tax assessment details, sales history, and related geographical information. The primary functionality of this table is to store and manage parcel data, while its secondary functionality is to provide information to support real estate transactions, land use decisions, and tax assessment purposes.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications for this table include, but are not limited to:
1. Real estate market analysis: Analyzing property values, sales trends, and regional differences to make informed investment decisions.
2. Urban planning and development: Identifying land use patterns and areas for development or conservation, evaluating environmental impacts and assessing the mix of residential, commercial, and industrial properties.
3. Tax assessment and administration: Calculating property taxes, assessing exemptions or special property classes, and maintaining tax records for local governments.
4. GIS applications and spatial analysis: Mapping parcels and analyzing their spatial relationships with other geospatial datasets such as zoning, transportation, or environmental data.

Keywords: Florida, parcels, real estate, land use, property value, tax assessment, sales history, geospatial, owner details, census block, GIS, urban planning, tax administration
