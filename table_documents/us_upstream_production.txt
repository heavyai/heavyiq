Description:
The "us_upstream_production" SQL table contains information on upstream oil and gas production wells in the United States. The table provides details on well identification, location, depth, production, and financial aspects, as well as the well's type, status, and operator information. The primary functionality of this table is to store comprehensive data on various aspects of oil and gas well production, while the secondary functionality is to enable efficient analysis of production trends, operator performance, and resources management for decision-making and optimization purposes.

Applications:
Potential use cases and applications for the "us_upstream_production" table include:
1. Understanding the geographic distribution of oil and gas wells across the United States.
2. Analyzing historical production trends of oil and gas wells, and identifying key factors impacting these trends.
3. Developing resource optimization strategies based on well location, depth, production rates, and financial metrics.
4. Comparing the performance of different operators and understanding their respective contributions to oil and gas production.
5. Supporting environmental impact studies by analyzing the production of oil, gas, and water at different wells, along with the well types and their locations.

Keywords:
Upstream, Production, Oil, Gas, Wells, Operator, Geographic Distribution, Resource Optimization, Historical Trends, Environment Impact

Column descriptions for us_upstream_production table:
- us_upstream_production.api_no_10: The unique API number assigned to the well.
- us_upstream_production.current_operator_name_full: The name of the current operator of the well.
- us_upstream_production.well_no: The numeric identifier assigned to the well.
- us_upstream_production.well_name: The name of the well.
- us_upstream_production.state_name: The name of the state where the well is located.
- us_upstream_production.county_name: The name of the county where the well is located.
- us_upstream_production.basin_play_name: The name of the basin or play where the well is located.
- us_upstream_production.field_name: The name of the reservoir or field where the well is located.
- us_upstream_production.well_type: The type of well (oil, gas, oil & gas, other, or cyclic steam).
- us_upstream_production.well_status: The current status of the well (active, shut-in, plugged, abandoned, or other).
- us_upstream_production.surface_lat: The latitude of the wellhead location.
- us_upstream_production.surface_lon: The longitude of the wellhead location.
- us_upstream_production.depth_tvd: The depth to the top of the reservoir in feet.
- us_upstream_production.horizontal_length: The horizontal length of the completed wellbore in feet.
- us_upstream_production.hole_direction: The direction of the wellbore (vertical, horizontal, directional, sidetrack, or unknown).
- us_upstream_production.date_spud: The date that drilling commenced.
- us_upstream_production.date_first_comp: The date that the well was first completed.
- us_upstream_production.production_first_date: The first date that the well began producing hydrocarbons.
- us_upstream_production.production_latest_date: The most recent date on which the well produced hydrocarbons.
- us_upstream_production.date_prod: The date of the monthly production record.
- us_upstream_production.production_month: The month of the monthly production record.
- us_upstream_production.oil_monthly: The volume of oil produced by the well in barrels during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.gas_monthly: The volume of gas produced by the well in Mcf during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.water_monthly: The volume of water produced by the well in barrels during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.oil_price: The price of oil in dollars per barrel during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.gas_price: The price of gas in dollars per Mcf during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.value_monthly: The value of the produced hydrocarbons in dollars during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.gor_monthly: The gas-oil ratio, calculated as the ratio of gas production to oil production during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.gor_log_monthly: The logarithm of the gas-oil ratio during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.gor_monthly_change: The monthly change in the gas-oil ratio during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.gor_monthly_change_log: The logarithm of the monthly change in the gas-oil ratio during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.water_cut_monthly: The water cut, calculated as the ratio of water production to total production during the production month.
- us_upstream_production.producing_formation: The name of the formation from which the well produces hydrocarbons.
- us_upstream_production.basin_boundary_geojs: A JSON string representing the boundary of the basin or play where the well is located.
- us_upstream_production.current_operator_name: The abbreviated name of the current operator of the well.
