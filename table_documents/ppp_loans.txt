Description: The ppp_loans table is designed to store information about loans granted under the Paycheck Protection Program (PPP). The primary functionality of the table is to maintain records of loan details, borrower information, loan status, and loan amounts. The secondary functionality includes tracking the loan's purpose, such as payroll, utilities, rent, and other expenses, as well as demographic information about the borrowers, such as race, ethnicity, gender, and veteran status.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications of the ppp_loans table include:
1. Analyzing the distribution of loans across different industries, regions, and demographic groups.
2. Evaluating the effectiveness of the PPP in supporting small businesses during economic downturns.
3. Identifying trends and patterns in loan approvals, disbursements, and forgiveness.
4. Assessing the impact of the PPP on job retention and business survival rates.
5. Monitoring the performance of lenders participating in the program.

Keywords: Paycheck Protection Program, PPP loans, loan approval, loan disbursement, loan forgiveness, borrower demographics, small business support, job retention, lender performance, economic relief.

Column descriptions for ppp_loans table:
- ppp_loans.LoanNumber: Unique identifier for each loan.
- ppp_loans.DateApproved: Date when the loan was approved.

- ppp_loans.ProcessingMethod: Method used to process the loan.

- ppp_loans.BorrowerCity: City where the borrower is located.

- ppp_loans.BorrowerZip: ZIP code where the borrower is located.
- ppp_loans.LoanStatusDate: Date when the loan status was last updated.
- ppp_loans.LoanStatus: Status of the loan.
- ppp_loans.Term: Length of the loan in months.
- ppp_loans.SBAGuarantyPercentage: Percentage of the loan guaranteed by the SBA.
- ppp_loans.InitialApprovalAmount: Initial amount approved for the loan.
- ppp_loans.CurrentApprovalAmount: Current amount approved for the loan.
- ppp_loans.UndisbursedAmount: Amount of the loan that has not yet been disbursed.
