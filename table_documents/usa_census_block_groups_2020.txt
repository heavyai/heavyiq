Description: The usa_census_block_groups_2020 table contains demographic and geographic data for census block groups in the United States, based on the 2010 and 2012 population census. The primary functionality of this table is to store and organize information about population, age distribution, race, household composition, housing units, and geographical attributes such as area and coordinates. The secondary functionality is to enable analysis and visualization of this data for various applications.

Applications: Potential use cases and applications for this table include:
1. Analyzing population distribution and demographics across the United States.
2. Identifying trends in population growth, age distribution, and racial composition.
3. Supporting urban planning and development initiatives by providing insights into household composition and housing units.
4. Facilitating the creation of maps and visualizations to display demographic and geographic data.
5. Assisting in policy-making and resource allocation by providing a comprehensive view of the population and its characteristics.
