Description:
The "us_upstream_production_sample" table contains information about upstream oil and gas production in the United States. It includes data such as API number, well details, location, production dates, monthly production volumes, prices, and other related information. The primary functionality of this table is to store and organize data related to oil and gas wells, their production, and associated parameters. The secondary functionality is to provide a basis for analysis and reporting on oil and gas production trends.

Applications:
1. Oil and Gas Industry Analysis: The table can be used to analyze and evaluate the performance of oil and gas wells, fields, and operators. It can help identify trends, production patterns, and potential areas for improvement or investment.

2. Production Forecasting: By analyzing historical production data and well parameters, the table can be used to forecast future production volumes and estimate reserves. This information is valuable for planning and decision-making in the oil and gas industry.

3. Regulatory Compliance: The table can be used to track and monitor compliance with production regulations and reporting requirements. It provides a centralized repository of data that can be easily accessed and audited.

4. Market Analysis: The table can be used to analyze the impact of production volumes, prices, and other factors on the oil and gas market. It can help in understanding supply and demand dynamics, pricing trends, and market competitiveness.
