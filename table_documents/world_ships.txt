Description: The "world_ships" table serves as a comprehensive database of various ships, their identification details, and specifications. The primary functionality of the table is to provide detailed information about each ship, such as maritime identification numbers (MMSI, IMO, and ENI), name, country, callsign, vessel type, vessel type-specific, gross tonnage, deadweight, TEU, dimensions (length and breadth), home port, year built, and current status. This allows for easy organization and retrieval of specific ship data.

Applications: Potential use cases for the "world_ships" table include assisting maritime authorities in monitoring and managing vessel traffic, providing data for ship tracking and analysis, and aiding shipping companies in planning and decision-making processes. The table data can also serve as a valuable resource for research and academic purposes, such as studying trends in shipping industries, analyzing the correlation between ship size and fuel efficiency, or investigating the impact of various vessel types on the environment.

Keywords: Maritime, vessel identification, ship specifications, maritime authorities, vessel traffic management, shipping industry, research, ship tracking, environment, fuel efficiency, vessel management.

Column descriptions for world_ships table:
- world_ships.mmsi: Maritime Mobile Service Identity of the ship.
- world_ships.imo: International Maritime Organization number of the ship.
- world_ships.eni: European Vessel Identification Number of the ship.
- world_ships.name: Name of the ship.
- world_ships.name_ais: Ship name as received over AIS.
- world_ships.country: Country of the ship's registration.
- world_ships.callsign: Radio callsign of the ship.
- world_ships.vessel_type: General type of vessel.
- world_ships.vessel_type_specific: Specific type of vessel.
- world_ships.gross_tonnage: Gross tonnage is a measure of a ship's internal volume.
- world_ships.deadweight: Maximum weight of cargo that can be carried by the ship in metric tonnes.
- world_ships.teu: Number of twenty-foot equivalent units that can be carried by the ship.
- world_ships.length_: Length of the ship in meters.
- world_ships.breadth: Width of the ship in meters.
- world_ships.home_port: Port where the ship is registered or normally based.
- world_ships.year_build: Year the ship was built.
- world_ships.status: Current status of the ship.
