Description: The movie_details table serves the purpose of storing extensive information about movies, including both their descriptive and quantitative details. The primary functionality of this table is to maintain a comprehensive database of movies that can be utilized for analysis and retrieval purposes. The secondary functionality is to store additional attributes, such as the number of production companies, countries, and spoken languages associated with each movie.

Applications: The movie_details table can be employed for various use cases and applications, such as:
1. Movie recommendation systems that utilize the stored attributes to suggest movies to users based on their preferences.
2. Analyzing industry trends over time, by aggregating data on budgets, revenues, genres, and more.
3. Creating visual representations of the movie data, such as charts or graphs depicting the relationship between budgets and revenues, popularity and vote count, or genres and production countries.
4. Identifying patterns and relationships among the movies, such as popular genres or production companies.

Keywords: Movie Database, Movie Information, Analysis, Recommendation Systems, Industry Trends, Visualizations, Movie Attributes, Relationships, Patterns.

Column descriptions for movie_details table:
- movie_details.id: Primary key for the movie.
- movie_details.budget: The budget of the movie.
- movie_details.genres: The genre(s) of the movie.
- movie_details.imdb_id: The ID of the movie on IMDb.
- movie_details.original_language: The original language of the movie.
- movie_details.original_title: The original title of the movie.
- movie_details.overview: A brief overview of the movie. 
- movie_details.popularity: The popularity score of the movie.
- movie_details.production_companies: The production companies associated with the movie.
- movie_details.production_countries: The production countries of the movie.
- movie_details.release_date: The date when the movie was released.
- movie_details.revenue: The total revenue earned by the movie.
- movie_details.runtime: The duration of the movie in minutes.
- movie_details.spoken_languages: The languages spoken in the movie.
- movie_details.status: The current status of the movie.
- movie_details.tagline: The tagline associated with the movie.
- movie_details.title: The title of the movie.
- movie_details.vote_average: The average vote score given by users.
- movie_details.vote_count: The total number of votes received.
- movie_details.num_production_companies: The number of production companies associated with the movie.
- movie_details.num_production_countries: The number of production countries of the movie.
- movie_details.num_spoken_languages: The number of spoken languages in the movie.
