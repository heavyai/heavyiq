Description: The "usa_states" table is designed to store detailed demographic, geographic, and statistical information about the US states. The primary functionality of this table is to hold an extensive range of data, including population and age distribution, the number of households, and occupancy types for each state. In addition to demographic data, the table also contains geographic data, such as area, length, and multipolygon geometry. Secondary functionality includes providing information on the agricultural aspects of each state, like farm counts, average sizes, and crop acres.

Applications: Potential use cases of the "usa_states" table are diverse, including:
1. Analyzing and visualizing demographic and geographic data for various research purposes, such as urban planning, transportation, and healthcare.
2. Comparing population density and age distribution of different states and regions for economic analysis and business location planning.
3. Conducting environment and agriculture-related studies to understand the potential impact of various policies on land use, crop yield, and farm sizes.
4. Identifying trends and patterns in population and household data for political analysts and policymakers.

Keywords: demographics, population density, households, age distribution, occupancy, agriculture, geographic data, land use, regions, multipolygon geometry
