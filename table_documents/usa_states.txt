Description: The usa_states table is designed to store detailed demographic and geographic information about each state in the United States of America. It contains data on population, ethnic composition, age distribution, household information, agriculture, and spatial features such as area, length, and shape of each state. The table's primary functionality is to provide a comprehensive repository of data pertaining to the states in the USA, and its secondary functionality is to enable efficient geographical and demographic analysis.

Applications: Potential applications of the usa_states table include, but are not limited to, conducting demographic studies, making data-driven policy decisions, supporting educational initiatives, managing territorial disputes, evaluating socio-economic conditions, and conducting market analysis for business expansion. Furthermore, it can be used for urban planning, environmental studies, public health assessments, electoral district allocations, and disaster response.

Keywords: demographics, geography, population, ethnicity, households, agriculture, United States, spatial analysis, regional analysis, socio-economic conditions, urban planning, public health, electoral districts, environmental studies
